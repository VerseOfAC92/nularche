Nularche Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (storage and encryption)

Nularche is a free, open-source notes app that runs on your own computer. It is made by VerseOfAC (verseofac@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Nularche, including when you connect Google Drive or Google Calendar.

The short version

  • Nularche has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We never receive your notes, files, calendars, passwords or Google data.
  • Everything Nularche stores is on your computer, in the folder you choose, as ordinary files.
  • When you connect Google Drive, Google Calendar, Apple iCloud Calendar or an AI service, Nularche on your computer talks directly to that service. Nothing passes through us.

Data stored on your computer

  • Notes, attachments and settings are plain files in your Nularche folder (your "Origin").
  • Sign-in tokens for Google and app-specific passwords for Apple are encrypted with your operating system's credential store (Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows, libsecret on Linux) and stay on that computer. They are never written to your notes folder or sent anywhere except to the service they belong to.
  • AI keys you enter (for example a Gemini API key) are stored the same way.

You can delete all of this at any time by disconnecting the account in Settings and deleting your Nularche folder.

Google Drive

If you choose Google Drive as storage, Nularche asks for the drive.file permission only. With it, Nularche can see and change only the Nularche folder and the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Nularche uses this access solely to keep your Nularche folder on your computer and the Nularche folder in your Drive in sync, so that the same notes appear on your other computers. Files you delete in Nularche are moved to your Drive trash; you can restore them from there.

Only your own content is synced (notes, attachments, settings, comments, named versions, PDF highlights and flashcard review state). Automatic version history, encrypted snapshots, AI records and calendar caches stay on each computer. Locked notes stay encrypted in Drive, and when you lock a note its unencrypted earlier copies are deleted from Drive for good. If you turn on end-to-end encryption, every file is encrypted on your computer with a key derived from your passphrase before it is uploaded; the passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere, and without it neither Google nor we can read the files.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Nularche asks for permission to read your calendar list and to read and change events. Nularche uses this access solely to show your calendars on its Calendar page and to create, edit or delete events when you do so in Nularche. If you choose to sync a Nularche table with a calendar, dated rows and events are kept in step. Event data stays on your computer (in your Nularche folder) and in your Google Calendar.

Use of Google user data

Nularche's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the Drive and Calendar features you turn on, inside the app on your computer.
  • Google user data is not transferred to us or to anyone else, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.
  • No person reads your Google data. Nularche has no servers, so there is no one at Nularche who could.

Apple iCloud Calendar

If you connect an Apple iCloud calendar, Nularche uses the app-specific password you create to talk to Apple's calendar server (CalDAV) directly from your computer, for the same purposes as Google Calendar above.

AI features

Nularche AI works with the AI service you choose. In automatic mode, the text of your request and the notes involved are sent from your computer to Google's Gemini API with your own key. In subscription mode, your Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini app reads and edits notes through a local connection on your computer. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies. Nularche never sends your notes to an AI service unless you make a request.

Other connections

  • Nularche checks GitHub for new versions of the app. This request contains no personal data.
  • YouTube videos you embed in a note are loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain when you view the note.

Children

Nularche is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Nularche does not collect anyone's data.

Changes and contact

Nularche Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe özet)

  • Google Drive: Nularche yalnızca drive.file iznini ister; Drive'ınızda yalnızca kendi oluşturduğu Nularche klasörünü görür, diğer dosyalarınıza erişemez. Bu erişimi yalnızca notlarınızı bilgisayarlarınız arasında eşitlemek için kullanır. PDF vurguları ve bilgi kartı tekrar durumu da eşitlenir; şifreli anlık görüntüler (Strata) ve otomatik sürüm geçmişi bilgisayarda kalır. Kilitli notlar Drive'da da şifreli kalır; isteğe bağlı uçtan uca şifreleme açılırsa her dosya yüklenmeden önce yalnızca sizin bildiğiniz parolayla şifrelenir.

  • Google'dan alınan veriler, Google API Hizmetleri Kullanıcı Verileri Politikası'na (Sınırlı Kullanım koşulları dahil) uygun olarak yalnızca bu özellikler için kullanılır; kimseye aktarılmaz, reklam için kullanılmaz, satılmaz ve yapay zekâ eğitiminde kullanılmaz.
