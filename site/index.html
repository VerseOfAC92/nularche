The idea

Null + Arche: start clean, build on first principles

I

Zero point

Nothing between you and your thoughts: no account, no lock-in, no feed. Just a folder of Markdown files that any editor can open, today and in twenty years.

II

Arche

Give every area of your life its own root, an Arche, and let pages, databases, manuscripts and boards grow from it.

III

A web of knowledge

Links, citations, live queries and flashcards turn notes into knowledge you can find, cite, reuse and remember.

Vision

Knowledge that belongs to the person who wrote it: readable without any app, private by default and useful for a lifetime.

Mission

Build a complete, beautiful notes studio on plain files, with AI you already own, no subscription and nothing to trust but code anyone can read.

How Nularche uses Google data

Google Drive

If you choose to keep your notes in Google Drive, Nularche asks only for the drive.file permission. It can see and change only the Nularche folder and the files it creates, never your other files, and uses this access solely to keep your notes in sync between your computers. With end-to-end encryption on, files are encrypted on your computer before upload, so not even Google can read them.

Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Nularche reads your calendars to show them on its Calendar page and creates, edits or deletes events only when you do so in Nularche.

Nularche talks to Google directly from your computer. Your Google data is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, never sold and never used to train AI models. Nularche's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
